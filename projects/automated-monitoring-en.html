From scattered monitoring to actionable alerts

A lightweight approach to bringing recurring checks together, comparing values and publishing a useful summary to the team channel.

Google Apps ScriptGoogle ChatMonitoringOperational reporting

Context

Application monitoring can rely on several signals: business indicators, scheduled jobs, observability alerts and tickets. When information is scattered, teams spend time finding it and comparing results manually.

Objective

Build a simple checking and notification workflow that highlights meaningful changes without introducing another complex platform.

Functional design

01

Collect

Read authorised sources and signals such as indicators, job statuses or operational tracking data.

02

Compare

Compare current values with the latest known values and surface meaningful differences.

03

Qualify

Highlight priorities, distinguish useful information from noise and keep the message readable.

04

Notify

Post a concise summary to a collaborative channel with enough context to start an investigation.

Intended outcome

More visibilityA clearer entry point for recurring checks.
Fewer manual checksAutomate repetitive collection and comparison tasks.
Faster responsePresent discrepancies with context to support decisions.

This case study describes the workflow and operational intent. No quantified time savings or incident reduction is claimed because no validated public measurement is available.
