PIAF — making tickets easier to work with

A support-team-focused cockpit prototype designed to reduce friction when reading, navigating and analysing tickets.

ITSMHTML / JavaScriptWorkflow designSupport experience

The observation

ITSM platforms retain the official record of incidents and requests, but everyday tasks can still be cumbersome: finding context, separating backlog from active work, reviewing history, preparing a customer-facing reply or analysing closed tickets.

The proposed response

Create a complementary interface that improves reading and navigation while keeping the official ITSM platform as the system of record. The prototype focuses on usability and operational shortcuts, not on replacing the existing platform.

01

Work views

Status and category filters, with a visual distinction between backlog and active flow.

02

Richer context

History review and quicker access to information useful for diagnosis.

03

Communication

Prepare requester-visible replies, with translation assistance when needed.

04

Analysis

Explore historical tickets to identify trends and support continuous improvement.

Design principles

  • Keep the ITSM platform as the official source and respect its traceability rules.
  • Do not expose ticket data to unauthorised people.
  • Never store secrets, tokens, cookies or credentials in the Git repository.
  • Make filters and actions understandable, with explicit limitations.

Value to validate

Faster reviewUX goal: reduce unnecessary navigation between views.
Better traceabilityBring the context and actions needed by support into focus.
Knowledge captureMake historical analysis and sharing lessons easier.

PIAF is presented as an internal tooling prototype. Real screenshots, ticket data, customer names, identifiers and proprietary integrations are intentionally omitted. Benefits are functional goals, not measured outcomes.
