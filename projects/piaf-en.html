PIAF — a cockpit designed for support teams

A complementary interface prototype to make tickets easier to read, recover context and simplify everyday work.

MY ROLEProduct design / Frontend prototype
SCOPEIncident and request management
TOOLSServiceNow (source officielle) · HTML / CSS · JavaScript
REAL EXCERPT · CROPPED IMAGE
IN THIS CASE STUDYThe contextThe challengeWhat I worked onDocumented figuresWhat I learned

The context

ITSM tools are essential for incident traceability, but their interfaces can slow down simple tasks: reading history, separating backlog, preparing a reply or filtering tickets.

The challenge

Design a more direct experience for support teams without replacing the official ticketing system or losing traceability.

What I worked on

01

Simplify views

Status filters, backlog separation and faster access to active tickets.

02

Preserve context

Review history and access information relevant to diagnosis.

03

Improve communication

Assist with replies and translation when needed.

04

Review history

Explore closed tickets to identify patterns and improvement opportunities.

Documented figures

A cockpit prototype with filters, history review and assisted-reply concepts. No quantified productivity gain has been validated.

Figures are from shared operational reviews. Their scope and permission for public use must be verified before publication.

What I learned

A useful tool respects the system of record while removing user friction. PIAF is presented as a prototype; no quantified productivity gain is claimed.
