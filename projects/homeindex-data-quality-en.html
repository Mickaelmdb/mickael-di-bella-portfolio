The context

In an application and data environment used by multiple business entities, support handles varied requests, calculation anomalies and interpretation questions. The need was to improve traceability, prioritisation and repeatable diagnosis.

The challenge

Move from reactive ticket handling to clearer operations, supported by shared metrics, useful documentation and better communication between support, technical experts and business teams.

What I worked on

01

Track the flow

Structure requests, priorities, categories and backlog monitoring.

02

Investigate

Use SQL / BigQuery, logs and ticket evidence to analyse anomalies.

03

Capture knowledge

Document procedures and share operational knowledge.

04

Measure

Track resolution time, ticket volumes and operational trends.

What I learned

Reliable operations depend as much on coordination and documentation as on technical tools. A useful investigation should be understandable and repeatable by someone else.
