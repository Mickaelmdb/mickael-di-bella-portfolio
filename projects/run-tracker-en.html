RUN Tracker — understanding operational workload

An activity-tracking prototype designed to make operational data easier to analyse and turn into decisions.

ReportingActivity trackingData visualisationAI-assisted synthesis concept

The need

A RUN activity report should do more than collect rows of data. It should help teams understand where time is spent, how workload is distributed, which categories require the most effort and where processes can improve.

Design directions

01

Structure activities

Classify effort by product, category, person and period, while allowing categories to evolve.

02

Avoid misleading views

Use averages and relevant comparisons rather than raw totals that hide the real distribution.

03

Improve reporting

Provide charts, summaries, exports and a quick view of the latest declarations.

04

Assist analysis

Explore a generated summary based only on available data, keeping assumptions distinct from facts.

Design principles

  • Make activity entry easier and allow users to correct their own declarations.
  • Avoid redundant inputs when measuring duration.
  • Keep charts readable and exports reusable.
  • Clearly separate calculated figures, interpretation and generated recommendations.

Expected value

Clearer workloadVisualise activity distribution and trends.
Better-supported decisionsIdentify pressure points from structured data.
Usable reportingReduce manual synthesis effort and improve sharing.

This case study describes design goals and features considered or prototyped. No quantified productivity gain is claimed.
