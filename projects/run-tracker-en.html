The context

A RUN review should not simply add durations: it should explain how effort is distributed by person, product, category and period. The team also raised concerns about cumbersome duration and occurrence inputs.

The challenge

Make tracking simple enough to be adopted while providing useful views for coordination and continuous improvement.

What I worked on

01

Simplify input

Reduce redundant fields and let users correct their own activities.

02

Evolve categories

Allow teams to add products and activity types without an administrator.

03

Make figures readable

Explore views by person, product and period with meaningful averages.

04

Improve summaries

Explore charts, exports and data-grounded assisted summaries.

Documented figures

A prototype and proposed improvements around simpler entry, categories and summaries. No rollout or quantified gain is claimed.

Figures are from shared operational reviews. Their scope and permission for public use must be verified before publication.

What I learned

Reporting quality starts with data quality and easy entry. This case describes requirements, prototypes and improvement ideas, not a confirmed production rollout or quantified results.
